Municipal Boundaries:
1- no drawing outside the municipal boundaries.
2- every element must have a name with a decision that has a date and a number

Areas:
1- scattered areas are not urban areas, therefore no drawing is allowed there besides of public builings

Districts:
1- no rules for districts for the meantime.

Roads:
1- all roads must have at least on connection to another road.
1- no road shall turn over 90°.

House Numbering:
1- the direction must be on one of two cases depending on the existence of the city center (origin of numbering):
	a- true: from the city center to the edges of Area.
	b- false: either East to West or from North to South (depending on the angle of the road).
2- numbering is linked to roads and separated, therefore its independent in every road.
3- numbering of main entrances starts from the beginning of the road to until the end with:
	a- houses on the left have odd numbers and starts from 01.
	b- houses on the right have even numbers and starts from 02.
4- numbering of secondary entrances is linked to main entrances and separated, therefore its independent in every main entrance.
5- numbering of secondary entrances starts from 01 and adds by 01.
6- numbering of secondary entrances must include the word "BIS" before the number.

Public Buildings:
1- no rules for public buildings for the meantime.

Public spaces:
1- no rules for public spaces for the meantime.
